Project Title: Build a simple 3D printed CNC plotter machine
Original Creator: Mert Kilic
Platform: PCBWay Community
Original Project: https://www.pcbway.com/project/shareproject/Build_a_simple_3D_printed_CNC_plotter_machine_dfd8b2d7.html

Reference and Modeling Approach
The original project by Mert Kilic was used as a reference for developing the 3D-printed mechanical components of our CNC Pen Plotter.
All parts documented in this repository were modeled in SolidWorks based on our project's mechanical requirements. The reference design was used to understand the original component concepts, while the models were recreated and, where necessary, redesigned to fit our machine's structure, dimensions, and hardware.
Some components retain similarities in shape or dimensions to the reference design, while others have been substantially redesigned to suit our specific assembly and functional requirements.

Major Redesigned Components
The following components were fully redesigned in SolidWorks for our machine:
- ElectronicsHousingA.STL and ElectronicsHousingB.STL – Newly designed electronic housings developed to fit our machine's electronics and available mounting space.
- pen_lift_slider_x1.STL – Redesigned pen-lift slider adapted to our machine's mechanical structure and movement requirements.
- servo_pen_lift_x1.STL – Redesigned servo-operated pen-lifting component adapted to our servo, pen-lifting mechanism, and assembly requirements.
Other components were also modeled or adjusted as necessary to achieve compatibility with our machine.
